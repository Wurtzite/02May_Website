**University of Nebraska Medical Center, College of Public Health** | Fall 2026 | 3 credit hours | Cross-listed as HPRO 906

This course builds the skills to design and deliver public health teaching and training. Topics include adult learning theory, instructional design, needs analysis, training evaluation, and building teaching modules for in-person, synchronous, and asynchronous settings. Across the semester, I develop one public health training module and adapt it for each of those three settings.

## DrPH Competencies

| Competency | Assignments that demonstrate it |
|---|---|
| **DrPH F18.** Assess an audience's knowledge and learning needs | Needs Assessment Plan; Evaluation Assignment |
| **DrPH F19.** Deliver training or educational experiences that promote learning in academic, organizational, or community settings | In-Person, Synchronous, and Asynchronous Public Health Teaching/Training Projects |
| **DrPH F20.** Use best practice modalities in pedagogical practices | Seven Principles of Adult Learning; Teaching Philosophy Statement and worksheets; Topic/Instructional Analysis; Objectives and Alignment; Digital Portfolio |
